PICO 2 W / LEON CUPRA DNUC: PROPOSED ISOLATED NET AND PIN REFERENCE
Reviewed: 2026-10-17. Architecture only, not assembly-ready wiring.
Detailed protection values and electrical/vehicle acceptance remain open.
See protection-plan.md, module-build.md, transport-options.md and
../docs/hardware/pico2w-board-audit.md. Mostly ready-made modules are selected;
no custom PCB, backup battery or automatic CAN-disconnect chain is selected.
Compact arrangement approved: modules near display, approximately <=1 m OBD
lead and <=20 cm encoder lead. Keep local 5 V/UART short; verify heat/clearance
and signal integrity. Capacitor parts/offers and placement: capacitors.md.

IDENTIFIED PARTS
===============
Owner photos: Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W mounted on PIM715 Display Pack 2.8;
Longan Serial CAN Bus V1.4 Aug 2020 (kit bag Seeed 114991438), MCP2515,
16.000 MHz crystal, SIT65HVD230 transceiver; Pololu 3782 D36V28F5;
Adafruit 3218 covered switch. Existing encoder: Adafruit 5880.
Longan MCU/LDO markings and V1.4 circuit remain unresolved. Older TI
SN65HVD230/MCP2551 schematics do not establish this board's rail or pin limits.

POWER DOMAINS (OWNER-SELECTED WIRED BASELINE)
==========================================
VEHICLE domain: OBD-side protection, Pololu, Longan, isolator input supply.
DASHBOARD domain: isolated supply secondary, Pico, PIM715, encoder, USB.
No direct VEHICLE_GND-to-DASHBOARD_GND connection across the barrier.
Mounts, shields, switch LED and test instruments must preserve that separation.

OBD 16 -> connector-adjacent fuse -> coordinated automotive protection
       -> Pololu VIN -> 5 V rail (independent OVP decision still open)
       -> Adafruit covered switch -> switched vehicle-side 5 V

Switched 5 V -> Longan branch (V1.4 supply/logic admission still required)
Switched 5 V -> regulated isolated DC/DC -> source-blocking external diode
            -> Pico VSYS pin 39 (secondary rail limits still require checks)
USB VBUS pin 40 -> existing onboard diode -> Pico VSYS pin 39

OBD 4 chassis ground and OBD 5 signal ground remain distinct named inputs.
Finalize their vehicle-side return topology before assembly. Do not route load
or surge current through a signal-reference wire. Neither joins Pico/USB ground.
This reference intentionally does not invent a final ground-tie location.

The Adafruit 3218 is specified for 12 V maximum, recommended 1-2 A despite
its 20 A case marking. Use protected low-voltage switching/control, not raw
vehicle battery. Its LED and return stay in the same selected power domain.
Approved location: regulated vehicle-side 5 V, before Longan/converter split.
Verify contact current/inrush; LED may be dimmer. Pololu stays enabled at its
documented 2-3 mA no-load draw, plus any input-protection consumption. Unplug OBD
for long parking or physical CAN disconnection. Keep switch off while starting
the engine, then switch on. Clean restart on supply dips is acceptable; retain
smoothing capacitors without a battery/supercapacitor ride-through system.
Pololu EN is pulled to VIN through 100 kohm: never connect it directly to GPIO.

A dashboard cutoff does not cut USB power. A separate Bluetooth dongle also
stays powered at OBD16 unless its own feed is disconnected. Capacitor energy
means actual shutdown time must be measured. A supply switch does not clear
an actual CAN short. 3V3_EN and RUN are not complete system power disconnects.

Never connect OBD16 to Longan VCC. Never directly join Pololu output to Pico
VSYS while assuming the switch blocks USB backfeed. Use the designed isolated
power path, or keep all vehicle-side power/signals disconnected during USB work.
The old 3 A fuse / SS34 / SMAJ14A / 500 mA PTC list is NOT an approved circuit.

Bulk candidates, pending inrush/damping and converter checks:
  Protected Pololu input: Panasonic FR 100 uF / 63 V EEUFR1J101L.
  Isolated output: Panasonic FR 220 uF / 50 V EEUFR1H221.
Each connects across its own supply and return with correct polarity, never
across isolation grounds. Keep fitted local ceramic bypass/storage. Do not put
bulk caps on CAN/I2C signals or treat the capacitor rating as a rail clamp.

CAN AND UART (PROPOSED)
======================
OBD 6  CAN-H -> matched CAN protection -> Longan CAN-H
OBD 14 CAN-L -> matched CAN protection -> Longan CAN-L
Longan TX -> vehicle-to-dashboard isolator -> Pico GP1 UART0 RX (pin 2)
Pico GP0 UART0 TX (pin 1) -> dashboard-to-vehicle isolator -> Longan RX

Use two opposite-direction channels with qualified idle-high, power-off and
brownout behavior. Vehicle-side supply/thresholds must match actual V1.4 rails;
dashboard side is 3.3 V. Do not bridge the isolated grounds or use an unqualified
I2C level shifter for push-pull UART. Isolator output idle does not guarantee
CAN recessive state or protect against a faulty adapter/transceiver.

SIT65HVD230: 3.0-3.6 V transceiver supply, +/-18 V DC bus absolute maximum;
manufacturer lists no dominant timeout. Select protection from SIT evidence,
not TI part similarity or a generic "24 V CAN TVS" description. These limits
do not prove the assembled module survives automotive transients.

Inspect P1 and measure CAN-H-to-CAN-L resistance on the DISCONNECTED, UNPOWERED
module. Published Longan pages disagree about default termination. Do not add
a third 120 ohm terminator to an already terminated vehicle bus. Never measure
resistance on an energized circuit; use moulded connector numbers and continuity
to resolve mating/solder-side views rather than assuming cable colours.

The optional longan-rx12 firmware expects externally provisioned 115200 8N1.
It neither scans baud nor provisions module EEPROM. It is an unqualified raw
CAN bridge path with no enabled diagnostic polling, not an ELM327 parser and
not a hardware listen-only guarantee (CAN ACK/error activity can still occur).

PICO 2 W / PIM715 EXPOSED HEADER PINS
===================================
Function                      GPIO             Physical pin
----------------------------- ---------------- ---------------------
Optional UART0 TX / RX         0 / 1            1 / 2
Encoder Qw/ST SDA / SCL        4 / 5            6 / 7
Buttons A / B / X / Y          12 /13 /14 /15   16 /17 /19 /20
LCD DC / CS                   16 /17           21 /22
LCD SPI0 SCK / TX              18 /19           24 /25
Backlight PWM                 20               26
LCD TE                        21               27
External RGB R / G / B         26 /27 /28       31 /32 /34
Pico RUN / LCD reset           RUN              30
3V3 OUT / 3V3 EN               supply/enable    36 /37
VSYS / USB VBUS                supply           39 /40
Ground                        GND              3,8,13,18,23,28,38
Analog ground                 AGND             33

GP2/3/6/22 have no named pack/application use. GP7-11 reach the SP/CE expansion
and are only spare while it is unused. RGB traces are assumed intact. GP21 is
not spare; optional TE observations do not prove panel presence or scanout.
Qw/ST powers encoder VIN at 3.3 V; use its ground only in DASHBOARD domain.
400 kHz I2C, module 10 kohm pull-ups plus HAL weak pull-ups require real cable
capacitance/rise-time and ESD checks. Do not feed the Qw/ST signals with 5 V.

W INTERNAL RESOURCES (NOT GENERAL HEADER PINS)
=============================================
GP23 = wireless power-on; GP24 = wireless data/IRQ; GP25 = wireless CS.
GP29/ADC3 = wireless clock and shared VSYS/3 sensing. Future ADC access must
coordinate with the radio and documented chip-select state.
WL_GPIO0 = onboard green LED; WL_GPIO1 = SMPS mode; WL_GPIO2 = VBUS sense.
The application uses none of these internal pins and initializes no radio.
PIM715's GP26-28 RGB is a separate LED. RP2350 core voltage is not WL_GPIO1.

CONTROLS AND ACCEPTANCE
======================
Boot brightness Full. Dashboard encoder click cycles Full / Dim (50% duty) /
Off; Off waits for its two-second popup and completed clear frame. Rotation
switches views. Protected two-second Y hold restores Dashboard at Full.
The backlight is approximately 1 kHz from the resolved clock; check its actual
waveform, pulse width, load/brightness and profile stability on the display.

Before vehicle use: verify fuse/wire/transient coordination, resolve rail OVP,
isolation, USB power ordering, switch behavior, regulated rail maxima, current,
inrush, temperature, UART logic margins, termination and CAN power-off loading.
Measure each retained module rather than using the old partial current totals.
Keep stock as default; the owner's working 280-70/1.30 V is an unqualified
overclock, and 290-72 failed on that device. Wireless operation adds a future
power/thermal/resource qualification task, not an automatic feature here.
